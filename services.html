FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do future predictions?

No. Future predictions are not in my scope. I focus on clarity, patterns, and grounded guidance. Solar Return readings or Dasha readings may be useful for energy assesment for future influences.

Can I request something specific?

Yes. If you want a custom reading or particular focus, message me and I’ll check if it fits my work.

What’s the difference between natal, solar and astrocartography?

Natal is about your core chart and life themes. Solar return reading is about the energetical influences within one chosen year, between Your birthdays. Astrocartography is about how different locations activate parts of your chart, used for relocation and location choice.

How long does it take to get a reading?

Response time and delivery depend on the service and complexity. I’ll confirm timing when you book.

What is the pricing for Your services?

Due to the nature of my services, I am unable to provide a set price for all of my services, since a lot is dependent on individuals and their needs. I'll provide You with pricing after inquery. You are also free to propose an amount that works for You.
