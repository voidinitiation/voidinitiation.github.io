      About VOID ✠

      VOID INITIATION is a Liberation Project. Here, we break free from Matrix in a real way, by exploring the ways in which we are collectively influenced. VOID is about combining science with spirituality, and coming up with a larger picture of life and society. In the world where distraction is the way those in power make us incapable of making any meaningful change, where humans are kept in a state of a dream world, where human psychology is used as a weapon, where we are given artificial replacements of human values behind a pay wall, where majority of the people lack basic self awareness in order to make better decisions for themselves... VOID is here to wake You up, to make You aware, to show you other choices, and it's possibly in this way, that we can collectively make a change.
