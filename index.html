      About VOID ✠

      See VOID's socials

      VOID INITIATION is a Liberation Project made to break ourselves free, by exploring the ways in which we are collectively influenced. VOID is about combining science with spirituality, and coming up with a larger picture of reality. In the world where distraction makes us incapable of making any change, where humans are kept in a dream world, where psychology is used as a weapon, where we are given artificial values behind a pay wall, where people lack self awareness in order to make better choices... VOID is here to wake You up, to make You aware, to show you an alternative path, and it's possibly in this way, that we can collectively change.
